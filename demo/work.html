Independent Ventures & Public Tools

Building with agency and public purpose
Open Public Project

Free Driving Test Routes

While helping a friend practice around the Dun Laoghaire test centre in Dublin, I was surprised to find that test routes were paywalled. Driving test routes are public information and should belong to everyone. I collected the open municipal data, mapped each route myself, and published the tool free online so learner drivers across Ireland can prepare without paying fees.

Trauma Informed Redesign

Post Conversation Survey Redesign

Discovered that positive support conversations were sometimes receiving low ratings. Thematic analysis revealed that asking sensitive Adverse Childhood Experiences questions immediately following a crisis conversation overwhelmed young people. Redesigned the survey questions and data structures. Initial study shows an increased response rate along with higher response volume while protecting vulnerable users.
