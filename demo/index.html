Dublin, Ireland • Digital Studio Founder • Community Listener

I work where organisations, evidence, technology, and people meet.

For over 13 years, my work has been about taking noise, cluttered systems, and difficult problems, and turning them into quiet clarity. I believe that people and organisations usually have the answers inside them already. What they lack is someone who will listen without judgment, strip away the clutter, and build practical solutions that truly help.
