Chapter 04 • Life Beyond the Screen

Where the noise gets to zero and answers begin to speak.

Water has always been my source of calm and perspective. Being near open water—whether kayaking along Dublin's coastline, watching the tide, or quiet mornings by a lake—brings a stillness where mental clutter clears away.

"Still water reflects clearly because it is not agitated. The mind works the same way: remove the noise, and clarity follows naturally."

Kayaking & Coastal Waters

Kayaking along the Irish coast provides physical rhythm and mental focus. Being out on the water reinforces the discipline of steady, patient navigation in changing conditions.

🧘

Vipassana Practice & Trust Governance

Practicing silent Vipassana meditation taught me observation without immediate reaction. Having completed multiple 10-day and 6-day residential courses, I was later appointed to the Irish Vipassana Trust committee, contributing governance support across finance, planning, and technology.

🌱

Food Rescue Volunteer (OLIO)

Believing in direct local action to reduce food waste, I volunteered as a Food Waste Hero with OLIO, collecting surplus groceries from local supermarkets and redistributing them to neighbors across the local community.

☘️

St. Patrick's Festival & Community

Volunteered on the ground for Dublin’s iconic St. Patrick’s Festival Parade, supporting crowd safety, route operations, and visitor guidance during Ireland's largest national cultural event. Having travelled across 20 countries, I value local civic contribution and global community connection.

Whether you want to discuss data strategy, digital tools, board governance, or simply connect over tea or a paddle on the water, I am always open to thoughtful conversations.
